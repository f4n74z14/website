Of course, my experience isn't limited to just one language, though it remains my favorite.  
For example, this blog brings together Astro, JS, CSS, HTML, design, writing, git, and Cloudflare Pages.  
The source code is completely open on [GitHub](https://github.com/f4n74z14/website).

My favorite color scheme is [Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com/), and the best monospace font is [PragmataPro Mono Liga](https://fsd.it/shop/fonts/pragmatapro/).  
I prefer working without font smoothing, going for pixel-sharp glyphs instead.

My approach is pragmatic, without fanaticism. My PC has served me since 2019, and its specs are:
- Inno3D GTX 1660 Ti;
- Intel i5 9400F;
- 32 GB RAM;
- Seasonic SSP 650W;

I believe that if a game or program won't run on hardware like this, then I'm simply not the target audience for that product.  
System requirements aren't just words—they're a guest list.  
And my name isn't on it.

I consider myself a humanist. I believe there is no good or bad outside my own mind: I'm the one coloring everything around me.  
That's where my neutrality comes from: not being let into a program doesn't mean the program is bad.  
And a program not being bad doesn't mean it's meant for me.

I enjoy experimenting. Whenever a new technology comes along, I'm happy to test it out—whether it's [local models](https://www.liquid.ai/) running on my GPU, or [voice input](https://handy.computer/) replacing the keyboard.

I prefer repairing rather than replacing, at least up until fixing costs more than buying new. For example, I picked up an [Ardor Gaming Ulta](https://ardor-gaming.com/product/29431034/mysh-besprovodnaya-provodnaya-ardor-gaming-ulta/) mouse  
because it tackles the wearing-microswitches issue head-on: hot-swappable sockets and 10 spare switches in the box.  
It's unlikely to go out of style—sure, it's not the flashiest thing now that magnetic switches exist, so let's see which one lasts longer.

I'm convinced that someone, someday will find this interesting to read. I'm not chasing a single narrow blog topic; I just want to share things and preserve what I've built and learned.

Thanks for stopping by.
